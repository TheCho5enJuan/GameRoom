How to play
  1. Hider: drag the figure, pinch it to resize/rotate, sample colors and paint camouflage.
  2. Seeker: drag to pan, pinch to zoom and quick-tap the painting to guess.
  3. Roles alternate each round. The host's Settings determine timers and penalties.
