Simple rules.
Big moves.

01 / STRETCH FOUR
Each turn, place one band along four consecutive pegs in a straight line. Every band after the first must connect to the existing network and cover a new edge.

02 / CLOSE TRIANGLES
Complete the three sides of a small triangle to capture it. One band can complete multiple triangles.

03 / WIN THE BOARD
Capture more triangles than your opponents. The game ends when 21 tokens are claimed by a player, the board has no available moves, or the chosen band limit is reached.

On your phone: tap two highlighted endpoint pegs, preview your band, then tap Place Band.
