How to play Wallbound

Race your pawn to the opposite edge before your rival gets there.

1
Move or build
On your turn, either move one space or place one wall. You cannot do both.
2
Move orthogonally
Pawns move up, down, left, or right through open edges. They never move diagonally normally.
3
Jump your rival
If the other pawn is directly beside you, jump straight over it when the space behind is open. If that jump is blocked by the board edge or a wall, you may step around either open side of that pawn.
4
Walls redirect, never seal
Walls span two spaces. They may not overlap or cross. Every wall placement must leave both players at least one route to their goal edge.
5
Reach the far edge
Player 1 wins by reaching the top row. Player 2 wins by reaching the bottom row.
