How to play

Reach the opposite edge before your rival.

1
Choose one action

Every turn, either move your pawn or place one wall.

2
Move through open edges

Move one square up, down, left, or right. Walls block movement.

3
Jump an adjacent rival

Jump straight over them when the square behind is open. If the jump is blocked, step around either open side.

4
Walls redirect, never seal

Walls span two spaces, cannot overlap or cross, and must leave both players a route to their goal.

5
Reach the far edge

Player 1 wins at the top row. Player 2 wins at the bottom row.
