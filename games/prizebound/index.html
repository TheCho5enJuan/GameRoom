Prizebound

A two-player race through a living fantasy world.

Roll the die!

How to play

Take turns rolling. First to reach the Grand Prize wins. The camera stays in the mode you choose.

Power spaces

⏪ Send Back — roll again; your rival moves back that many spaces.

🛡️ Block — your rival loses their next turn.

🔄 Odd or Even — choose, then roll. Correct swaps you with the player ahead; wrong swaps with the player behind.
