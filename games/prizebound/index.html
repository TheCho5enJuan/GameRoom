Prizebound

A two-player race through a living fantasy board.

Roll the die!

How to play

Take turns rolling. Your piece moves along the path. First to reach the Grand Prize wins.

Power spaces

⏪ Send Back — roll again; your rival moves back that many spaces.

🛡️ Block — your rival loses their next turn.

🔄 Odd or Even — choose, then roll. Correct swaps you with the player ahead; wrong swaps with the player behind. If nobody is in that direction, nothing happens.
