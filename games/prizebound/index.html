How to play

Take turns rolling. First to reach the Grand Prize wins.

MAP stays zoomed out. FOLLOW tracks the active piece. Dragging or zooming switches to EXPLORE.

⏪ Send Back: roll again and push your rival back.

🛡️ Block: your rival loses the next turn.

🔄 Odd/Even: predict the second roll to attempt a position swap.
